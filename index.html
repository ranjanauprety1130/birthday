My wish for you

For the woman who makes every moment a little more alive.

Dear Ranjana,

Before you came into my life, everything was simple and predictable. I was the good son everyone loved, nobody really scolded me, and at work I was the dependable one who got things done and found a solution whenever something went wrong. I was appreciated everywhere, and my days were mostly books, work, home, and repeat. There was no one to argue with, no one to fight with, and honestly, not much chaos either.

Then you arrived, and life became far more interesting. You brought laughter, arguments, little fights, conversation, and the kind of lovely chaos I never knew I was missing.

Whatever life brings us, the quiet days, the disagreements, the adventures, and everything in between, I want to spend it with you. I would choose this beautiful, unpredictable life with you every time.

With all my heart, Happy Birthday ♡
